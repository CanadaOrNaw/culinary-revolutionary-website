Tour of Spain

A four-course Spanish menu moving from tapas-style starters through soups and salads to regional mains and classic custards.

Price depends on the number of courses you choose and your guest count.

Starters

Choose 1
  • Patatas Bravas Crispy fried potatoes topped with a spicy tomato sauce and often garlic aioli
  • Croquetas Creamy, breaded croquettes filled with choice of ham, chicken, cod, or mushrooms.
  • Pan con Tomate Toasted bread rubbed with ripe tomato, garlic, olive oil, and salt.
  • Gambas al Ajillo Shrimp cooked in olive oil with garlic and chili flakes.
  • Pimientos de Padrón Small green peppers blistered in olive oil and sprinkled with flaky salt.
  • Albóndigas Meatballs served in a rich tomato or almond-based sauce.
  • Boquerones en Vinagre Fresh anchovies marinated in vinegar, garlic, parsley, and olive oil.
  • Pulpo a la Gallega Tender octopus with olive oil, paprika, and coarse salt over sliced potatoes.
  • Jamón Ibérico Thin slices of premium dry-cured Iberian ham served with bread.
  • Aceitunas Aliñadas Mixed olives seasoned with herbs, garlic, citrus, and olive oil.
  • Champiñones al Ajillo Mushrooms sautéed with garlic, olive oil, parsley and white wine

First course

Choose 1
  • Gazpacho Chilled tomato and vegetable soup
  • Salmorejo A thicker, creamier tomato soup, topped with hard-boiled egg and cured ham.
  • Sopa de Ajo Rustic soup made with garlic, bread, paprika, and poached egg.
  • Ensalada Mixta Lettuce, tomatoes, onions, tuna, olives, and hard-boiled eggs.
  • Espárragos con Vinagreta Asparagus served with a tangy vinaigrette and chopped vegetables.
  • Judías Verdes con Jamón Green beans sautéed with garlic and cured ham.
  • Menestra de Verduras A medley of seasonal vegetables, lightly stewed.
  • Arroz Caldoso A comforting rice dish with chicken in a flavorful broth.
  • Pimientos Rellenos Roasted peppers filled with ground pork and beef
  • Crema de Calabaza Smooth pumpkin soup flavored with olive oil and spices.

Mains

Choose 1
  • Paella Valenciana The iconic rice dish made with chicken, rabbit, green beans, and saffron. (4-8 guests only)
  • Pollo al Ajillo Chicken browned and simmered with garlic, olive oil, white wine, and herbs.
  • Bacalao al Pil-Pil Salt cod cooked in olive oil with garlic until the sauce becomes silky and emulsified.
  • Cordero Asado Slow-roasted lamb seasoned simply with garlic, rosemary, and olive oil.
  • Fabada Asturiana A hearty white bean stew with chorizo and pork.
  • Merluza a la Vasca Hake cooked in a light sauce with garlic, white wine, peas, and asparagus.
  • Rabo de Toro Slow-braised oxtail in a rich red wine and vegetable sauce.
  • Fideuà A seafood dish similar to paella but made with short noodles instead of rice
  • Chuletas de Cordero Grilled lamb chops seasoned with garlic, herbs, and olive oil.
  • Pollo en Pepitoria Chicken braised in a traditional sauce made with saffron, almonds, garlic, egg yolks, and white wine. It's a historic Castilian dish known for its rich, nutty flavor.
  • Lubina a la Sal Whole sea bass baked in a thick crust of sea salt, then cracked open and served with olive oil, lemon, or simple herb sauces. The salt crust keeps the fish exceptionally moist and flavorful.

Dessert

Choose 1
  • Flan A silky baked vanilla custard topped with caramel sauce.
  • Arroz con Leche Creamy rice pudding flavored with cinnamon, lemon peel, and vanilla.
  • Crema Catalana A smooth citrus- and cinnamon-infused custard with a crisp caramelized sugar topping.
  • Tarta de Santiago A naturally gluten-free almond cake flavored with lemon zest and dusted with powdered sugar.
  • Natillas A simple stovetop vanilla custard, garnished with cinnamon and a crisp cookie.
